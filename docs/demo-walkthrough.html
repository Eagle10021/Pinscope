Signal generator

SignalGenerator.ino · 115 200 baud

A function generator for a lab oscilloscope: square wave on pin 11 (1 Hz – 100 kHz, timer hardware so no jitter), PWM on pin 9, and sine / triangle / sawtooth on pin 6.

On the board
In the simulator

ExpectPin 11 reads 1.00 kHz; A0 swings about 0–5 V at 10 Hz.

RC charging

RcCharge.ino · 115 200 baud

Charges a capacitor through a resistor and plots the curve. τ = R × C = 1 kΩ × 10 µF = 10 ms; after one τ the capacitor is at 63% (3.16 V).

On the board
In the simulator

Expect63% after 10 ms; nearly full after 50 ms (5τ).

Button bounce

ButtonBounce.ino · 115 200 baud

Counts how many edges one press makes. Metal contacts bounce, so a fast microcontroller sees several presses.
